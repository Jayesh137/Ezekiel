KEY TRADING PRINCIPLES
A distillation of GCR&apos;s most impactful trading insights
Low Unit Bias
The gravitational power of low unit bias on retail is the most powerful magnet in crypto. Why would anyone buy less than a tenth of a Bitcoin when they could own millions of dog coins?

Schelling Points
Round numbers are Schelling points—either potential support or resistance, especially when there&apos;s no rationale for the valuation.

Never Short Small Caps
Never short micro to small caps. If GCR follows this rule with conviction, you surely should too. The supply can be cornered after years of seller exhaustion.

Momentum Persistence
What is strong tends to remain strong, and vice versa. Cutting your winners to add to your losers is a bad strategy—you would usually be better off doing the opposite.

Fading DOGE Pumps
From the SNL × Musk show onward, fading every DOGE pump was one of the highest hit-rate trades available. The enthusiasm of the runup can never live up to expectations.

Intuition Over Analysis
GCR&apos;s edge has nothing to do with drawing lines on charts or complex strategies. His edge is intuition. The best traders prioritize intuition over apophenia.

New vs Old Coins
New coins have an advantage over old coins: lots of hope, lack of bagholders, team still not rich and incentivized to create hype.

News Reaction Trading
The way the market reacts to news gives you information on bias and mood. More often than not, the actual veracity of the headline is immaterial—it&apos;s how the market reacts, and for how long.

Mental Cleansing
Cleanse your mind of your peak net worth. Eternal sunshine of the spotless mind.

Risk Timing
During alt cycles, maximize risk at the beginning and slowly reduce over time. Most people do the opposite—slow early and increasingly greedy with time.

Asia Will Lead
China and Asia in general will fuel the next bull run. The East is ascending and yearning to flex.

Bottom Signal
GCR flipped bullish after FTX debacle, believing we bottomed in November 2022. He did all his buying around 16-18k.

Long-Term Conviction
Most people with long-term conviction would be better off holding BTC and ETH rather than trading. ETH at 10k is one day programmed.

Don&apos;t Catch Knives
Do not try to catch knives. You may be down a lot of money and view it as a way to average down—it almost never works out.

Networking Edge
If you don&apos;t have an edge in trading, try going all out in networking. Every conference, be there. In bear markets, humans are much more accessible.
